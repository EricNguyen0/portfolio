## About

I'm Eric Nguyen, an early-career digital product professional with a mix of **product thinking, e-commerce, UX/UI, frontend development, and digital marketing**.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **design and technology** — understanding what a business needs, figuring out how a digital experience should work, and helping turn that idea into something people can actually use.

My experience includes working on e-commerce platforms, Shopify and WooCommerce stores, custom product configurators, website improvements, digital content, and product-focused projects.

Rather than focusing on just one part of the process, I like understanding the **bigger picture** — from the original business requirement all the way to the final user experience.
